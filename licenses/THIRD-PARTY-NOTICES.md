## Ink Bleed theme transition

The files in `lib/ink-bleed/` adapt the WebGL theme transition from
[codemure-official/ink-bleed-theme-changer](https://github.com/codemure-official/ink-bleed-theme-changer).
They are distributed under the MIT License; see
[ink-bleed-theme-changer-MIT.txt](./ink-bleed-theme-changer-MIT.txt) for the full
copyright and license text.

The transition uses the existing GSAP dependency and `html2canvas` for theme snapshots.
Those dependencies retain their own licenses.
